Earp Street Park

Hey, I’m Rob and I’ve been trying to keep this lot clean for all the kids that have moved in over the last few years, but I’d like to do a little more and breathe a little life back into our old Earp Street Playground - it might not be a playground anymore, but it can still be beautiful.

If you see a blonde guy with a tape measure and a sketchbook (or neck deep in weeds), come say hi! I have so many questions about this old lot, and I'd love to hear more about the history of the lot and the planters!

The Plan & The Ask

A fence, a sign, new boards, trees in the planters — and the people, materials, and truck to make it happen.

Open the plan ›

The plan

  • A fence along the street edge Finding some sort of fencing we can rig onto the metal fence posts. I’m thinking wood pallets could do the job nicely (and free!)
  • A “No Dumping” sign Our block captain can request one for free, but if we don’t have a captain then I’ll keep an eye on Facebook marketplace for one.
  • Fresh slats on the picnic table and benches We need some new wood for the benches and picnic table. They’re easy to replace and the current wood is too damaged to repair easily. Then we can paint them and get them looking nice! That’s 18 pieces of 2”x6” pressure treated lumber I’m looking for.
  • Trees in the existing planters Until I can get the Redevelopment Authority’s “permission” to do work restoring this lot as a park, we can’t request a tree from PHS. I’m working on getting “permission”, but until then if anyone knows of some free Fig trees or shade trees, let me know!

What it needs

  • People If anyone wants to help out, I’ll put together a day we can all go out and clean up the debris, weed the perimeter, maybe plant some things, maybe work on the table and benches, just let me know! I’d be grateful for any help - it’s a big lot!
  • Materials
    • Pressure treated lumber
    • Paint and paint brushes (no specific color, we’ll take what we can get and make it work!)
    • Wood pallets
    • Bollard caps
  • A truck Something with an open bed for one afternoon: scrap and old wood out, lumber and trees in. If you have one and a free afternoon, the form on this board works.

So I’ve heard a lot of stories about this old lot, how it used to be a playground, what it used to be for all the kids and the parents on the block. And I’m not here to turn it into anything that it already wasn’t - a clean, safe place for the kids that parents will feel safe with their kids playing at.

“You’ve gotta ask the older folks on the block about those planters, they’re older than me and ain’t nothing been planted in them since I’ve been alive.”
