Earp Street Park

Hey, we're Rob and Brian. We've been on the block a few years and we try to keep the abandoned lot on our block clean and weeded whenever we can. There's a few things there that we'd really love to restore like the picnic table, the two benches, maybe get something planted in those planters (we tried a blueberry bush and a blackberry bush, but those darn squirrels), maybe some fencing put back up on those old fence posts. This site is to find all of that info; what we're working on, things we're on the hunt for (like some pressure treated lumber and paint), and a contact form for any questions - though you can always just come up and chat with us.

Come say hi!

If you see a blonde guy with a tape measure and a sketchbook (or neck deep in weeds), come say hi! I have so many questions about this old lot, and I'd love to hear more about the history of the lot and the planters!
