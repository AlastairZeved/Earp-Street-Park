What We've Done

9/25/2026

The planters had gnarly weeds throughout. We cut them down and dug them up to prevent roots breaking the concrete.

Tree planting season is coming up — looking for appropriate small trees for large containers that offer shade or fruit (e.g., fig tree).

9/2/2026

Lots of weeding! I didn’t realize how quickly it can get overgrown across the fencing and in the planters. With Fall approaching, that means we’re almost in tree planting season - and how good would some fruiting or shade trees look in those planters? Fig trees have been on my mind lately.

5/10/2026

Planted a blueberry bush and a blackberry bush! The blueberry bush was from Farm Philly and the blackberry bush I pulled from my garden. Not sure how they'll survive with the squirrels, but we'll find out!

What We'd Like To Do

  • Put up a fence but not a tall fence, maybe hip height wood fence to help stop the dumping there
  • Put up a “No Dumping” sign (our Block Captain can request a free one)
  • Replace the wood slats for the picnic table and two benches, then paint/seal the wood so they last
  • Plant some container trees in the planters. Something that can provide some shade in the summer, but not large trees that can’t fit in a planter
  • Repair the cracked pavement across the lot (working on getting some grant money to pay for it)

What We Need

  • 18 pressure-treated slats, 2x6 10' long
  • Paint
  • A "No Dumping" sign (our Block Captain can request it)
  • Hip height fencing (maybe wood pallets would work?)
  • Container trees (trees that don't have sprawling roots)
